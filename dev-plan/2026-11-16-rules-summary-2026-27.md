## The table

Blank (—) means the cipher isn't used there. **Decode** means the key is given;
**Cryptanalysis** means the solver works it out (from a crib, the question's hints or letter
frequencies). Division A isn't split by level.

Notes:

- Xenocrypt counts (0–1, 1–2, …) are how many xenocrypts a test of that level has.
- 3.e rules apply at every level; 3.f rules are the ones added at state and national.
- Affine isn't in the table: it's no longer used.
- Toebes's own test checker enforces this table. A question outside it gets a warning: for example
  "Cryptanalysis problems are not allowed on Division B (Middle School) Regional/Invitational".
  It also flags things the table doesn't show: no misspelled aristocrats in Division B, and random
  alphabets for a misspelled aristocrat or a patristocrat.

## Gaps against what `prepare` supports today (first pass)

**Division C is out of scope** (owner, 2026-11-16), so gaps 1 and 2 are closed: not to be built.
The K2 decision in the competition-ready plan stands.

Supported now: aristocrat and patristocrat with random or K1 alphabets (aristocrat also in
Spanish), atbash and caesar (decode), Baconian (words, sequence, letter for letter), checkerboard,
Nihilist and homophonic (decode and crypt), Porta, complete columnar, fractionated Morse,
cryptarithm (addition only). Anything else renders as `unknown.typ`'s error box.

1. **Aristocrat K2 and K3, Division C.** Not supported. The competition-ready plan says "K2 won't
   be added", because K2 left **Division B**, but this table still has K2 and K3 in **Division
   C**. **For the owner:** is Division C in scope? If so, that decision needs another look.
2. **Aristocrat, keyword (Division C).** The student finds the keyword. It probably needs its own
   question text and an answer-key entry (the keyword, not the plaintext). Not supported.
3. **Division A types:** PigPen/Masonic, Knights Templar, Tap Code, Standard Galactic Alphabet and
   Vigenère. None is supported; the first four are symbol fonts or pictures. Whether Division A is
   in scope is the owner's call.
4. **Xenocrypt languages other than Spanish.** The table says "language" without naming one; we
   fold accents for Spanish only. Check what Toebes offers before building.
5. **Cryptarithm forms other than addition.** The table says only "base 10"; we handle A + B = C
   and A + B + C = D. Check which forms Toebes generates.
6. **Known loud failures that a valid question can hit:** fractionated Morse with digits, Baconian
   A/B sets with formatting (Division A's "4 or fewer symbols" may be exactly that), and a Baconian
   word that no longer fits its letter (Backlog: Toebes's fallback word).
7. **Decode vs. cryptanalysis variants.** We print what the export gives; whether each cipher's
   question text and paper differ correctly between the two (e.g. Porta, complete columnar) hasn't
   been checked against this table.
